Verify a TapeOut circuit before you trust it.

TapeOut turns transistors into on-chain circuits on X Layer. Anyone can deploy a look-alike contract — but only real processors are registered in the TapeOut factory. Paste a processor or circuit address and this page reads the chain directly and issues a passport: identity, owner, gate count, mint price, container status and plain-language safety checks for secondary buyers.

Reads happen in your browser against public X Layer RPC endpoints. Nothing is uploaded, cached or simulated — if a read fails, the passport says so.

What a passport can — and cannot — tell you

  • It proves registration. The factory's on-chain registry is the ground truth for "is this a genuine TapeOut processor". Imitation contracts fail that check.
  • It proves consistency. A genuine processor's circuit and transistor contracts point at each other, and minted supply never exceeds the cap — both are checked from live reads.
  • It is not an audit. A passport says nothing about whether a circuit's logic is correct, fair or safe to interact with — only what the contracts publicly expose.
  • Trust, but verify. This page reads through public RPC endpoints. For a high-value purchase, cross-check the same addresses on the OKLink explorer.
